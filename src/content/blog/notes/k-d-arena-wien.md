# Kruder & Dorfmeister concert in Arena Wien

So I went to the K&D concert in the Arena Wien yesterday, and what should I say, it was just amazing. a series of backflashes to my youth.
What amazed me the most was how music from 30 years ago still sounded futuristic today.
I kept thinking, if my parents had gone to a concert with a band from 30 years earlier, it would be music from another era, but this was still totally uptodate music.

more to come.
